# Delight and motion across public CMS blocks

The motion follows the existing Poppins, teal, gold, fine rules, and directional arrows. A single 700ms rule-drawing sequence marks the first call to action on the page. Quiet supporting responses acknowledge intent: arrow direction, button pressure, link underlines, linked photograph crops, FAQ disclosure, continuous team-card flips, gallery changes, clipboard confirmation, and actual form submission results. Informational copy stays visible by default; static sections do not acquire repeated entrance effects.

A shared activity observer stops text ribbons, partner-logo motion, and slideshow autoplay outside the viewport, on a hidden document, and under reduced motion. It responds to preference changes and cleans up observers, listeners, timers, and animation handles. Text and logo loops have explicit Romanian pause controls. Keyboard focus pauses moving partner marks and slideshow playback; duplicate logo sequences are inert. Reduced motion retains short color/confirmation feedback and provides a static, horizontally scrollable original logo list. No new dependency or raster asset ships.

Scope is public CMS presentation and existing interactions. Schemas, approved copy, page order, homepage records, saving, and publication were not changed. Earlier uncommitted changes were preserved using a pre-pass source snapshot. No type generation was necessary for this presentation-only pass.

Verification:
- 188 Chromium layout/content cases passed: 32 block variants at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px, plus 28 long/missing-content cases at 320px. No document overflow, malformed links, duplicate IDs, broken visible images, or browser exceptions in this fixture matrix.
- Eight existing interaction checks passed: FAQ and team keyboard control, gallery dialog navigation/Escape/focus return, horizontal keyboard scrolling, accordion-gallery arrows, download semantics, real clipboard copying, and required-field plus mocked form submission.
- Sixteen motion checks passed: visible playback, keyboard pause/resume, offscreen stop/return, hidden-document stop/return, live reduced-motion changes, independent repeated loops, one CTA sequence, interrupted CTA visibility, gallery autoplay/focus/offscreen behavior, clipboard denial/retry, form error then success, missing observer/animation APIs for the owned fallback paths, and visible static markup without animation JavaScript.
- Four focused final cases confirmed the form checkmark animation and paused/reduced logo behavior at 320 and 1440px after the finish batch.
- Text/background contrast sampling across 11 representative block surfaces found no misses. The new loop controls also exceeded 4.5:1 in playing and paused states. Sampling does not certify arbitrary CMS colors, every photographic/decorative pixel, or transition frames.
- Reviewed desktop/mobile captures for all 32 variants together, plus normal-motion interaction captures at 320 and 1440px. One finish batch stabilized the form-icon selector and scoped reduced-motion duplicate hiding; its affected states were captured and reviewed once more.
